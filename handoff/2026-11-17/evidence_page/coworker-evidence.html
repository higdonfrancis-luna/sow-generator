How we know

Our process,
start to finish.

Six steps, from reading Adobe’s pages to building the services into the SOW Builder. Each step shows the date and the numbers.

  1. Step 1 · November 16 and 17

    Read Adobe’s own pages

    We began with the 10 Adobe sources behind the Coworker case. Then we read 286 Adobe pages live and saved each one: all 80 pages of the Coworker guide, the Coworker pages in 14 product guides, 5 Adobe news releases and 2 Adobe developer pages.

  2. Step 2 · November 17

    Find the work each feature leaves to the client

    Our rule: list a service only when Adobe calls for the work directly, or when the work follows from what Adobe documents about the feature. We recorded 190 findings, each with an Adobe quote. In 94, Adobe calls for the work. The other 96 follow from a documented capability, limit, setting or data condition.

  3. Step 3 · November 17

    Write the service

    From those findings we wrote 26 new Coworker services, each with its Adobe page and quote. With the 34 already in the tool, that makes 60. Every one is in the tables below.

  4. Step 4 · November 17

    Check every quote word for word

    A script matched all 190 finding quotes word for word against the saved pages. For this page we checked all 63 quotes again, and all 63 match. We checked 62 against our saved live reads. The Adobe Support and Services site refuses automated tools, so we checked its quote against the page text the tool’s owner pasted. Only spacing and quote mark style may differ.

  5. Step 5 · November 6 to 17

    Try to prove them wrong

    A separate review reread the Adobe pages behind the new services live, 86 pages in all. For each service it checked the quote, the release status and any overlap with services already in the tool. It corrected 17, kept 9 and dropped none. Older services had earlier checks: a November 6 citation audit, and a November 12 outside review where a second checker tried to refute every claim that held.

  6. Step 6 · November 17

    Approve and build

    The tool’s owner approved the 26 new services, a Coworker + Adobe Analytics connection, six RFI write-ups and industry-neutral wording. We built them into a working copy of the SOW Builder and RFI Builder. A second review found no defects, and all 33 test suites pass. Once saved, all 60 services are in the SOW Builder and 59 have an RFI Builder write-up. Today the live tool has 34 of the 60.

- Brand Visibility and Brand Concierge integrations; support for agentic commerce protocols from Google and OpenAI; developer agents for app development and storefront or extension migrations.
- HIPAA-ready offering for Commerce on Cloud.
- No section on the page compares the versions (on-premises, on Cloud, Cloud Service, Optimizer).

# How partners will find ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot

November 18, 2026. Adam's question: "How will people know when to add ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot? ... If a partner doesn't know where to add them, then how can they add them? ... Do you think we should have some plays we add to our tool that are focused on MCPs? I think those are potentially big differentiators. I just don't know how to expose them to our partners."

Status: a recommendation with pictures. Nothing is built. Adam approves from the pictures first.

## The recommendation

1. **One new ready-made play in the agentic lineup: "Adobe in your client's AI assistant."**
   - The canvas starts with Coworker in the middle and Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude around it, the way the CDN sits on the Brand Visibility plays. The partner keeps the assistant the client uses.
   - The ranked Add product list, asked the play's own problem, suggests what else works in that assistant: Customer Journey Analytics, AEM Sites and Assets, Workfront, GenStudio for Performance Marketing. Each brings its line.
   - The conversation it opens: here is how the Adobe your client already pays for shows up in the assistant their people use every day. It leads to Coworker.
2. **A client problem on the first page,** in the client's words: "Our people work in Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT or Claude all day, and want our Adobe data and tools there." It opens the new play.
   - It also fixes a gap: today, typing "Copilot" or "Claude" on the first page lands on Brand Visibility, because the "missing from AI answers" problem lists those words. The new problem takes the "inside the assistant" wording; Brand Visibility keeps "missing from AI answers".
3. **A hint on every other canvas,** Create your own play included. A product that works in an assistant says so under its name ("Works in Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude"); clicking the line adds the assistant. In the Add outside system menu, the assistants the canvas's products work in come first.

## Money, honestly

The assistant lines sell few hours on their own (Adam: "it's a value add and the services will come somewhere else"). The play earns through Coworker, the Adobe products it pulls in, and the rollout services (8 to 48 hours each). If partners take to it, two more plays can follow the same pattern: content work in the assistant (AEM Sites and Assets, Workfront, Claude Design) and campaign work in the assistant (Target, Real-Time CDP, Journey Optimizer).

## Update, later on Nov 18: one complete agentic play (Adam's direction)

Adam did not follow the first answer above and asked for a "pure agentic play" instead, built on why any company uses AI: "find customers, keep customers, grow their share of wallet". He then set the product backbone: "Retention probably falls more to Adobe Journey Optimizer ... acquisition probably falls more to Real-Time CDP connecting to different advertising and media channels ... organic growth probably connects more to Adobe Target and Adobe Journey Optimizer ... CJA obviously feeds everything with all of the data." He wants the existing plays left as they are ("I don't wanna screw that up"). He also forwarded ChatGPT's view, which reached the same three-way structure.

The proposal now (pictures `e1_agentic_explainer.png` and `e2_first_page_agentic_card.png`; script `agentic_mock.js`):
- **One new first-page card, "Adobe agentic AI", built like the GenStudio card:** three outcome areas and a button for the complete play.
  - Find new customers: Real-Time CDP (audiences to ad and media channels) and Brand Visibility (showing up in AI answers).
  - Keep customers: Journey Optimizer and Brand Concierge.
  - Grow share of wallet: Target and Journey Optimizer.
  - In every area: Coworker (Adobe's agents do the work) and Customer Journey Analytics (feeds everything with the data). The complete play adds Brand Intelligence and Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude.
- **Teach the AI connectors by example (from ChatGPT's view):** each assistant line opens with a question the team would really ask, then what Adobe does, then what the partner sets up. The explainer shows four, each within what Adobe's connector for that product does: Real-Time CDP (look only), Journey Optimizer (Claude only, look only), Target, Customer Journey Analytics.
- **Taken from ChatGPT's view as well:** keep and grow do not need a website rebuild, so Edge Delivery Services and Sites Optimizer stay with the existing Brand Visibility play; its five-minute "find" demo is a good partner script. Its demo step "Claude checks a graphic against brand guidelines" holds: Adobe's developer guide for Brand Intelligence (https://developer.adobe.com/adobe-brand-intelligence/guides/mcp-integration/) names Claude Desktop as the example host for Validate. So a Claude + Brand Intelligence line goes in after the three-box build (round one had held it back).
- The earlier single-assistant play ("Adobe in your client's AI assistant", pictures d1 to d3b) is replaced by this card. The "Works in ..." hints on products (d3, d3b) still apply to every canvas.

# How partners will find ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot

November 18, 2026. Adam's question: "How will people know when to add ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot? ... If a partner doesn't know where to add them, then how can they add them? ... Do you think we should have some plays we add to our tool that are focused on MCPs? I think those are potentially big differentiators. I just don't know how to expose them to our partners."

Status: a recommendation with pictures. Nothing is built. Adam approves from the pictures first.

## The recommendation

1. **One new ready-made play in the agentic lineup: "Adobe in your client's AI assistant."**
   - The canvas starts with Coworker in the middle and Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude around it, the way the CDN sits on the Brand Visibility plays. The partner keeps the assistant the client uses.
   - The ranked Add product list, asked the play's own problem, suggests what else works in that assistant: Customer Journey Analytics, AEM Sites and Assets, Workfront, GenStudio for Performance Marketing. Each brings its line.
   - The conversation it opens: here is how the Adobe your client already pays for shows up in the assistant their people use every day. It leads to Coworker.
2. **A client problem on the first page,** in the client's words: "Our people work in Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT or Claude all day, and want our Adobe data and tools there." It opens the new play.
   - It also fixes a gap: today, typing "Copilot" or "Claude" on the first page lands on Brand Visibility, because the "missing from AI answers" problem lists those words. The new problem takes the "inside the assistant" wording; Brand Visibility keeps "missing from AI answers".
3. **A hint on every other canvas,** Create your own play included. A product that works in an assistant says so under its name ("Works in Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude"); clicking the line adds the assistant. In the Add outside system menu, the assistants the canvas's products work in come first.

## Money, honestly

The assistant lines sell few hours on their own (Adam: "it's a value add and the services will come somewhere else"). The play earns through Coworker, the Adobe products it pulls in, and the rollout services (8 to 48 hours each). If partners take to it, two more plays can follow the same pattern: content work in the assistant (AEM Sites and Assets, Workfront, Claude Design) and campaign work in the assistant (Target, Real-Time CDP, Journey Optimizer).

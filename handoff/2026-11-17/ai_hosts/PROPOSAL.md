# ChatGPT and Claude as outside systems: the proposal

November 18, 2026. For Adam. Nothing is built. Everything waits for your yes on the pictures. This version was corrected after two independent reviews.

## The short version

- **Good catch.** Adobe documents ways to use 13 of the tool's 34 products from inside ChatGPT or Claude. The tool already sells two services for this, with no box to draw them from.
- **Add two boxes: ChatGPT and Claude.** Two, because Adobe documents them differently. Marketo Engage and Firefly Creative Production are documented for Claude and not for ChatGPT. Adobe Express is proposed for ChatGPT only.
- **21 new lines and 7 new services.** The tool goes from 162 to 183 connections and from 569 to 576 services.
- **Adobe Coworker play: yes, offer both.** Not switched on from the start.
- **Brand Visibility plays: no line to Brand Visibility, ever.** Brand Visibility measures answers in ChatGPT. That is not a connection. The boxes are offered on a play only when a product on it connects to them. Today that is the play with Customer Journey Analytics.
- **What else is missing:** Microsoft 365 Copilot is the next assistant to check. Most other gaps are missing lines from boxes the tool already has.

The other AI's research was a useful list of leads. About one in five of its 87 records held up as a connection on Adobe's own pages.

## How the money works

- **Services are listed under the Adobe product**, the same way "Coworker set up in an AI platform" is today. They show whether or not a box is on the canvas (picture 5).
- **The box shows the client where the work runs, and its card offers the service** with hours and a tick (picture 2b). That tick on a line's card is new.
- **Each service covers one assistant at small and typical size. A client with both ChatGPT and Claude is the large size.** The existing Coworker service already works this way.
- **Three new services overlap with Coworker services** when Coworker is on the same quote: Workfront (the same write switch), Adobe Analytics (the same permissions) and GenStudio for Performance Marketing (the same publishing rule). The tool's overlap review will flag each pair, and each new service says it reuses what the Coworker service already set.
- **Five lines sell no hours**: Creative Cloud, Acrobat and Adobe Express. A person adds Adobe's plugin themselves.

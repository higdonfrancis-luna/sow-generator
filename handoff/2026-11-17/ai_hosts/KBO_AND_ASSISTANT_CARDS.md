# Client KBO plays and AI assistant cards: my recommendation

Your guesses were good. Adobe's own pages back your backbone: Real-Time CDP finds customers, Journey Optimizer keeps them, Target and Journey Optimizer grow what they spend, and Customer Journey Analytics feeds all three. Two changes: **add GenStudio for Performance Marketing to Find new customers**, and **take Brand Intelligence out of the complete play**. Every quote below is Adobe's own words, proven word for word.

## Copilot, honestly

You're right: Copilot is the thin one. It has one strong story, Coworker in Microsoft 365, and the rest is set-up work.

- Ready-made Adobe connections a business can use, in each assistant's own list: Claude 8, ChatGPT 8, Copilot 1 (Coworker).
- Coworker in Copilot is good. The client's Microsoft 365 administrator approves it, and people ask about audiences, journeys and campaign results in Teams, Word, Excel and PowerPoint. Adobe lists Microsoft 365 Copilot first in Coworker's set-up steps ([Coworker in AI platforms](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/coworker/content/ai-platforms)).
- Everything else needs the partner: CJA and AEM as an agent built in Copilot Studio, Workfront and GenStudio by Microsoft's own steps. Adobe: "Microsoft controls the setup flow for custom MCP connections in Copilot." ([GenStudio](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/genstudio-for-performance-marketing/user-guide/ai-assistants/connect-ai-assistants))
- No Copilot connection reaches Real-Time CDP, Journey Optimizer or Target, the products behind the KBO plays. Copilot sees them only through Coworker's answers, and Adobe says "Coworker returns read-only insights and does not modify your customer data."
- My call: keep a Copilot card, last in the row, told as "Coworker in Microsoft 365, plus the set-up work". A client on Microsoft 365 still gets a starting card, and the Copilot Studio work is real partner hours.
- Two fixes before it ships: the five assistant services need the Copilot steps (today they describe ChatGPT and Claude only), and the Copilot + AEM Sites line should say Adobe's Microsoft route is an agent built in Copilot Studio ([AEM with Copilot Studio](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/experience-manager-cloud-service/content/ai-in-aem/mcp-support/enterprise-platforms/setup-microsoft-copilot-studio)).

## Good to know

- Requirements, kept with each product and never on a card: Target's product recommendations need Target Premium ("Recommendations activities are available as part of the Target Premium solution."); CJA's who-stays views come with CJA Select and up, or as an add-on ([guided analysis](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/analytics-platform/using/guided-analysis/overview)); Real-Time CDP's prospect audiences need the client's own data partner ("you license prospect profiles from one or more data partners").
- Business-to-business clients: the three areas are built for consumer brands. For a B2B client the Add product list brings Marketo Engage, Journey Optimizer B2B Edition and Marketo Qualifier. Adobe ties Journey Optimizer B2B Edition to "acquisition, expansion, and retention strategies" ([Journey Optimizer B2B Edition](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/journey-optimizer-b2b/user/guide-overview)).
- Hours: GenStudio adds about 272 typical one-time hours and 20 a month to Find (its own set-up, plus its links to Real-Time CDP and Coworker). The complete play nets about 120 more one-time hours (GenStudio in, Brand Intelligence out).
- Evidence: all 25 Adobe quotes on this page are proven word for word on Adobe's own pages, and all 118 quotes from the two research runs were proven again (132 different quotes in all). One new page was fetched: CJA's paid media guide. Nothing in the tool was changed.

                          Core Value Proposition
                          The Adobe B2B Commerce play addresses the growing need for digital commerce
                          capabilities, helping businesses meet rising customer demand for online purchasing. It
                          tackles common challenges like managing complex product offerings, integrating legacy
                          systems, and meeting modern customer expectations for seamless, personalized
                          experiences. Adobe’s integrated solutions enable businesses to digitize every aspect of the
                          B2B process — from acquiring and onboarding customers to managing complex pricing,
                          product configurations, discounts and varying procurement approvals — driving growth
                          and operational efficiency. By streamlining these areas, companies can overcome internal
                          hurdles and deliver the online experiences today's B2B buyers expect.

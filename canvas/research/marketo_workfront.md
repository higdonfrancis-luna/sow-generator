## Connections with services

**marketo-workfront** (Workfront Fusion). Use Case Patterns: "Automate marketing campaign request intake and Marketo Engage program creation using Workfront forms and Fusion" and "Integrate Workfront proofing and approval workflows with Marketo Engage email assets using Fusion automation".
- Flow Workfront to Marketo: "pass them to the My Tokens in the Marketo Engage program" (Intake and Create).
- Flow Marketo to Workfront: "your scenario can retrieve the Marketo Engage email as an HTML file, zip it up, and save a copy" (Review and Approve).
- Flow Workfront to Marketo: "Workfront Fusion can detect when the email has been approved by stakeholders" (Review and Approve).
- XB01: "take information from the Workfront request and lookup the correct program templates" (Intake and Create).
- XB02: "Approve the Workfront Proof to trigger asset approval in Marketo Engage, then mark the task as complete." (Review and Approve).
- XB03: "you will need to build out your Programs and Program Templates in Marketo Engage to utilize My Tokens" (Intake and Create).
- Supporting: the Fusion Marketo modules include "Clone a Program", "Schedule a Campaign" and "Update a record".

**marketo-rtcdp** (standard edition; B2B Edition for all datasets).
- XB04 and Marketo to Real-Time CDP flow: "With the Marketo Engage source connector, you can bring B2B data from Marketo Engage to Experience Platform." Without B2B Edition only the "people and activities datasets" reach Profile.
- XB05 and Real-Time CDP to Marketo flow: "push audiences created in Adobe Experience Platform to Marketo where they will appear as static lists" (Marketo Engage destination). Account and prospect audiences are not supported.
- Marketo side: the Experience Cloud integrations page lists the "Adobe Real-Time CDP Marketo Connecter", which "can require mapping of the IMS org in Marketo Engage."

**marketo-ajo** (standard Journey Optimizer docs).
- XB06 and Journey Optimizer to Marketo flow: "A specific custom action is available in your journeys to integrate Adobe Journey Optimizer and Marketo Engage." Prerequisites: "The customer instance of Marketo Engage must be IMS-enabled." and MktoSync ingestion service access.
- B2B Edition (not used here): "Deploy Journey Optimizer B2B Edition with Marketo data for orchestrating buying group journeys and account engagement" (Use Case Patterns).

**marketo-cja.**
- XB07 and flow: "Use the Marketo Engage connector to bring data from Marketo to Experience Platform." then "establish a connection between datasets in Experience Platform and Customer Journey Analytics." B2B Edition optional: "You might consider the Customer Journey Analytics B2B Edition to get much more value from Marketo Engage data."

**marketo-target.**
- XB08 and flow: "Share Marketo list membership with Experience Platform or other solutions." The page lists Adobe Target as a destination and says a list "can also be accessed by Adobe Target" after it reaches Audience Manager. Needs "Set Up Adobe Organization Mapping"; not available for HIPAA-ready instances.

**marketo-eds** (AEM Forms as a Cloud Service needed).
- XB09 and flow: the Edge Delivery submit actions page lists "Submit to Marketo Engage" under "Submit Actions for Forms created in Universal Editor". The Configure Submit Actions page lists "Third-party service integrations (Marketo, Power Automate, Workfront Fusion)". aem.live Forms lists "Submit to Marketo Engage".
- Ruled out: Marketo's AEM Asset Selector, because "Marketo currently supports Adobe Experience Manager versions 6.4 and 6.5."

**marketo-cw.**
- How: Coworker "provides agent skills designed to automate time-consuming but important marketing functions." Skills: build programs ("complete with asset placeholders and scheduling"), investigate leads, validate programs ("surfaces issues prior to launch"), import leads.
- Marketo to Coworker flow: "processes standard B2B marketing data already present in the user environment" (data information sheet).
- XB10: "you must perform some setup steps to ensure the desired users have access". Provisioning "is limited to Marketo Engage users who have agreed to the Core Gen-AI terms."
- Coworker home page: Coworker Chat is available in Marketo Engage and Workfront, among others.

**marketo-bc.**
- How and XB11: Brand Concierge "closes the loop between your website and Marketo Engage", "flowing every visitor interaction into Marketo as native activity data", with "automatic lead creation". Activities can be used in Smart Campaigns as triggers, filters or batch data.

**workfront-eds** (AEM Assets as a Cloud Service is the documented target).
- How: the integrations overview supports "Adobe Experience Manager Assets as a Cloud Service" and "Adobe Experience Manager Assets Essentials"; it does not mention AEM Sites.
- XB12 and Workfront to AEM flow: "Push assets and metadata from Adobe Workfront to Experience Manager Assets". Configure page: workflows are "a set of actions that connect Workfront to Adobe Experience Manager as a Cloud Service"; metadata moves only from Workfront to AEM; "You must be a Workfront administrator."
- XB13 and Edge Delivery to Workfront flow: the Edge Delivery submit actions page lists "Invoke Workfront Fusion".

**workfront-cw.**
- How: "Coworker plans the work, executes it in Workfront or across your Adobe and connected systems." "Users can only take actions they're already permitted to take in Workfront." "Coworker has read-only access by default."
- XB14: "enable the Write MCP tools option in your organization's System Preferences" (Use CX Coworker in Workfront).
- Limit: "CX Enterprise Coworker is not currently available to organizations in health care, finance."

**workfront-ajo** (Workfront Fusion).
- XB15 and flow: the Journey Optimizer modules "allow you to create, read, update, or delete records", covering content templates and fragments, and messages. Requires "an active Adobe Journey Optimizer account" and a Workfront Fusion license.
- Not used: the One Adobe tutorial 1.3.4 routes Workfront approval through GenStudio for Performance Marketing ("Next, click Request approval, which will connect to Adobe Workfront."), not straight to Journey Optimizer.

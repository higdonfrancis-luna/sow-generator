# Partner services statement of work tool

## Working in this repository

The SOW Generator lives at the root of this repository. Earlier project notes use `warroom/sow/` paths. Drop that prefix when locating source files here. The built app opens at `site/index.html`; the Services and RFI page is `site/sow/index.html`.

Partners can start with one of three **Revenue growth goals** in the problem finder to preview a play for acquisition, retention or growing spend with existing customers. Each of the six GenStudio answers explains the connections, the work a partner can deliver, and the starting hours and fees. Partners can refine a recommendation before building it, return from a built canvas to its original answer, or clear the question and choose another.

From the repository root, rebuild the app and run the focused canvas checks:

```sh
python3 canvas/build.py
python3 build_site.py
node test_canvas_scope.js
node test_canvas_navigation.js
node test_canvas_authoring.js
```

The two browser suites use Playwright and Chromium. Offline builds use the recorded verification of the four existing security-overview quotations. To check new or revised quotations against the source PDF, set `SOW_SECURITY_OVERVIEW_PDF` to its local path before building.

A single HTML file partners open to scope the services behind a Brand Visibility play, see the hours, enter their own hourly rate and read what they'd make. It answers open item 8 of the Workstream 2 responses (Oct 27 2026): an editable SOW tool where a partner changes the scenario and the economics update live.

Adam's rules for it (Oct 29 2026):

- Six templates: the four sales plays in deck order (CJA, Commerce, AEM Sites on Edge Delivery, Brand Concierge for FSI), then "Brand Visibility on its own" and "Coworker on its own". The two on-its-own templates live only in this tool, not in the deck or the video.
- Partners care about the work and the hours. No software pricing anywhere in the tool; the partner enters a rate ($100 to $300 an hour, their call) and the tool does the math.
- Hours are starting estimates that change with the size of the customer. Partners change any line.
- 200 prompts is the starting point partners change. Adobe's docs recommend no number: onboarding generates a small set of prompts, trials include up to 100 (200 before April 1 2026), and the license is sold by the number of prompts in the sales order (sources below).
- Adobe red (#EB1000), black and white, Source Sans 3 like the video page, the Adobe logo in the masthead. The SOW document itself carries the partner's name and no Adobe logo: it's the partner's paper to its customer.
- Nothing about Adobe Consulting Services.

## How the numbers work

Every one of the 38 services has starting hours (`base`) and a planning range (`low` to `high`) in the catalog at the top of `index.html`. The customer-size switch (Small, Typical, Large) sets every unedited catalog line to its low, base or high hours across all six templates; a line the partner edits keeps its hours until "Restore starting scope and hours", and custom services keep theirs. The size is saved with the draft. A fee is hours times the partner's rate, which can be overridden on any line. One-time services add up once; monthly services multiply by the recurring months. The totals show one-time, monthly and the services total.

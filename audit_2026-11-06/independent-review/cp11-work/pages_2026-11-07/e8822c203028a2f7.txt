## Content Fragments with Adobe Journey Optimizer content-fragments-with-journey-optimizer 

Last update: October 9, 2026 

- Applies to: 

- Experience Manager as a Cloud Service 

- Topics: 

- Administration 

CREATED FOR:

 Adobe Journey Optimizer helps you deliver connected, contextual, and personalized experiences to your customers. By integrating Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) as a Cloud Service with Adobe Journey Optimizer (AJO), you can reuse AEM content in your AJO inbound channels, and your AJO outbound channels; including web, SMS, email, and others. 

For example, you can: 

- seamlessly incorporate your AEM Content Fragments into your Journey Optimizer email content 

- preview the AJO experience directly from AEM 

The connection between Content Fragments and AJO simplifies the process of accessing and leveraging AEM content, enabling the creation of personalized and dynamic campaigns and journeys. 

For details start with the AJO documentation: 

- Using Content Fragments in AJO 

- Integration AJO Offers with Content Fragment 

## Dispatcher Configuration dispatcher-configuration 

To allow AJO to access the AEM Content Fragments through the Content Fragment Management API , you need to configure the Dispatcher: 

- 

In dispatcher/src/conf.dispatcher.d/filters/filters.any : 

- 

Add: 

 code language-xml 

 # Allow Content Fragments API requests, required for integration with AJO
/200 {/type "allow" /url "/adobe/sites/cf/*" }

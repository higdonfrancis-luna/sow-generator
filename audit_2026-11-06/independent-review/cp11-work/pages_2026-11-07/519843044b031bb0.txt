## Snapchat Ads 

Last update: October 21, 2026 

- Topics: 

- Sources 

CREATED FOR: 

- Developer 

 NOTE 

The Snapchat Ads source is in beta. Read the Sources overview for more information on using beta-labeled connectors. 

 IMPORTANT 

The Snapchat Ads source is available as part of the Customer Journey Analytics SKU. 

The Snapchat Ads source is an Adobe Experience Platform paid media connector. Use it to connect your Snapchat ad accounts and ingest campaign, ad, and performance data, such as engagement metrics, into Experience Platform. 

The connector publishes the data that it captures to Paid Media datasets. Customer Journey Analytics uses these datasets to provide insights into your Snapchat marketing campaigns. 

To connect your account, you sign in to Snapchat and authorize Experience Platform to access your Ads Manager Organization. You do not need to generate or provide any API credentials manually. 

## How it works 

After you create a Snapchat Ads dataflow and select the ad accounts to target, Experience Platform runs the following jobs automatically. 

- If Paid Media datasets do not already exist for your organization, Experience Platform creates them. 

- A backfill job runs 30 minutes after you create the dataflow. It fetches campaign and ad metadata and metrics for the ad accounts for the previous 30 days, and publishes the results to the Paid Media datasets. 

- An incremental job runs once daily, starting the day after you create the dataflow. It fetches metadata and metrics for the previous day and publishes the results to the Paid Media datasets. 

- A restatement job runs after each incremental job. Restatement happens three times: one, two, and three days after the incremental job it corresponds to. 

## Prerequisites 

Before you connect Snapchat Ads to Experience Platform, ensure that you have the following: 

- An existing Snapchat ad account, or the ability to create one. 

- Campaigns and ads already set up in your Snapchat ad account. The connector reads from this existing advertising data. It does not create, modify, or delete Snapchat campaigns, ads, or other resources. 

## Schema and dataset provisioning 

The Snapchat Ads source assigns a target dataset, schema, and field mapping automatically. You do not select a target dataset or map fields yourself when you create the dataflow. 

The connector provisions one Paid Media dataset per entity type: account, campaign, ad group, ad, asset, and summary metrics. Read the following section for the core identity fields that the connector maps for each entity. 

## Snapchat Ads XDM schema 

Read this section for information on the core identity and status fields that the Snapchat Ads source maps to each Paid Media schema. Every mapped record also includes an entityType value that identifies the entity, and a paidMedia.adNetwork value of snapchat .

## Summary metrics fields 

The summary metrics schema stores performance data for your Snapchat accounts, campaigns, ad squads, ads, and creatives. Snapchat refreshes metrics approximately every 15 minutes, and finalizes non-conversion metrics 48 hours after the end of the day in the ad account timezone. 

Each metrics record maps to the identifier of the entity that it measures.

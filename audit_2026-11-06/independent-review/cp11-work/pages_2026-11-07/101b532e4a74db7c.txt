## CX Enterprise Coworker overview overview 

Last update: November 2, 2026 

CREATED FOR: 

- Admin 

- User 

- Developer 

- Leader 

Coworker is an AI-powered teammate that reimagines the nature of work for organizations, teams, and individuals. It automates customer experience and marketing workflows so organizations can focus on achieving business goals and improving outcomes rather than coordinating tasks. As an agentic engine, Coworker takes an innovative approach to business process automation. It improves AI model performance and accuracy by bringing together enterprise context, data, intelligence, collaboration, and the execution of agentic skills, with governance and human oversight built in. 

Coworker is built for organizations running complex, cross-functional customer experience workflows. It brings together Coworker Chat and Coworker Projects, enabling practitioners to move from a single task to a full, multi-team workflow within the Coworker experience. Governance, approvals, and human oversight are integrated from the start. Learn more on Adobe for Business .

## Coworker Chat 

Coworker Chat is a conversational interface for getting customer experience work done. Describe what you need in plain language, and Coworker Chat plans the work, runs it across your Adobe and connected systems, and returns the results for your approval. For more information, see the Coworker Chat overview .

## Enable Coworker Chat 

To enable Coworker Chat, your organization needs access to Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker. Enablement typically involves the following: 

- Licensing: Contact your Adobe account representative or sales team to confirm you have the right licenses and entitlements. 

- Onboarding: Adobe’s enterprise support or professional services team configures Coworker for your environment, sets up integrations with your Adobe applications, and defines governance and approval workflows. 

- Access: Assign roles and access to Coworker features for your teams in the Admin Console . 

After Coworker is enabled, see Get started with Coworker Chat to open Coworker Chat, send your first message, and review responses.

To connect Coworker Chat to an external tool or service, add it as a server under Customizations > User > Integrations . For the steps and a description of each field, see Connect a server . 

## Coworker for teams 

Coworker for teams is a standalone application for small, agile marketing teams who want to stand up and run campaigns quickly. It is not part of another CX Enterprise application. It is available as a free trial through December 31, 2026, in North America only. For access steps, see How to access . 

Coworker for teams includes connectors for Marketo Engage and HubSpot .

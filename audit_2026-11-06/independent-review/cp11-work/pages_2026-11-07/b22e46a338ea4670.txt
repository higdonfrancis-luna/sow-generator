## Overview of CX Enterprise Coworker in Workfront 

Last update: October 22, 2026 

- Topics: 

- Get Started with Workfront 

CREATED FOR: 

- User 

 IMPORTANT 

CX Enterprise Coworker is not currently available to organizations in health care, finance, or some other industries with sensitive data. AI Assistant is available to these organizations. For more information, see AI Assistant overview . 

CX Enterprise Coworker Chat is a conversational interface for getting work done, and is now integrated with Workfront. You describe a goal in plain language, and Coworker plans the work, executes it in Workfront or across your Adobe and connected systems, validates the results, and brings the finished work back to you for approval.​ 

Everything your team relies on in AI Assistant today continues to work, but now has more powerful, end-to-end capabilities, available both in a new full-screen experience and in the Workfront right panel. 

Coworker respects your organization’s existing Workfront-level access controls. Users can only take actions they’re already permitted to take in Workfront. Coworker has read-only access by default, and system admins control when users have Write access. 

Coworker is part of the Adobe ecosystem, and is not just limited to Workfront. 

For information on using Coworker in Workfront, see Use CX Enterprise Coworker in Workfront . 

For more information on Coworker and its capabilities, see Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker Chat overview . 

For skills available in Coworker in Workfront, see Workfront skills in CX Enterprise Coworker . 

For example prompts, see the prompts in the article Use the Adobe Workfront MCP server .

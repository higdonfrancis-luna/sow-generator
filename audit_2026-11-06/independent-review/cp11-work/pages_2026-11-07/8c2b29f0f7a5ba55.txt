## CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage Overview overview 

Last update: November 5, 2026 

CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage provides agent skills designed to automate time-consuming but important marketing functions. 

 AVAILABILITY 

This feature is available to all subscriptions. If you do not see the CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage tile on your My Marketo screen, contact your account manager. You must also agree to the Core Gen-AI terms and the supplemental terms . 

 IMPORTANT 

- 

After CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage has been enabled for your subscription, you must perform some setup steps to ensure the desired users have access. 

- 

Review data scope, governance controls, and PII considerations in the CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage data information sheet . 

## How to access access 

On your My Marketo screen, click the CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage tile. 

Type your request in the prompt field, select one of the agent skills, or try one of the sample prompts. 

## Skills skills 

The center console features a growing set of agent skills available to help you with various tasks. Each skill is a purpose-built AI assistant you interact with through natural language to complete a specific task. 

## Plan campaigns plan-campaigns 

Turn a campaign brief into a structured implementation plan for Marketo Engage. The Plan campaigns skill outlines program structure, audience logic, emails, Smart Campaigns, tokens, reporting, and pre-activation checks. Learn more about the Plan campaigns skill . 

## Build programs build-programs 

Describe a marketing campaign in plain language and CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage builds the program structure, complete with asset placeholders and scheduling. Learn more about the Build programs skill . 

## Investigate leads investigate-leads 

Find out why a specific person/lead did not reach a milestone (like MQL, program qualification, or a campaign) and get a plain-language explanation of what happened. Learn more about the Investigate leads skill . 

## Product knowledge product-knowledge 

Product knowledge gives you on-demand access to Marketo expertise without leaving the platform. Ask a question in plain language and CX Enterprise Coworker for Marketo Engage draws on official Adobe documentation to answer it. Learn more about the Product knowledge skill . 

## Validate programs validate-programs 

Validate programs automatically checks your setup against Marketo best practices and surfaces issues prior to launch. Learn more about the Validate programs skill . 

## Import leads import-leads 

Import and deduplicate lead lists into your Marketo Engage database with field mapping assistance. Learn more about the Import leads skill . 

## Coming soon coming-soon 

Additional skills designed to handle the most repetitive, time-consuming work are coming soon.

3.4 How would you connect our Customer Journey Analytics data to AI assistant traffic, and what do you need from us?

Drafted answer

Brand Visibility sends aggregate AI-agent activity from your CDN logs into Customer Journey Analytics through an Experience Platform source, and AI-referred visits and conversions come from CJA's own web data. We connect the two, build the CJA data view that maps page, referrer, country and device to visits and conversions, and rank the AI sources by what they send. The data is aggregate: nothing ties a person to an AI mention.

What we would need from you
  • A paid Brand Visibility offering and CDN access logs forwarded for each site
  • An Experience Platform user and a CJA administrator for the source dataflow, connection and data view
  • CJA Select, Prime or Ultimate for the derived field that groups AI referring domains
Services this answer draws on
  • Connect Brand Visibility and CJA measurement (one-time)
  • Rank AI sources by the visits and conversions they send (monthly)
  • CDN log forwarding, set up by the partner (one-time)

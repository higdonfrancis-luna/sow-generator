Your response to the client

Where your data is hosted

  • AEM Sites: you choose the primary region when the environment is created, for example the US, Canada, the UK, Germany, the Netherlands, Japan or Australia, as your entitlements allow. It cannot be changed afterwards. Pages are delivered through Adobe's managed CDN.1
  • Edge Delivery Services: content being worked on is held in the US and published content is distributed globally. For a specific jurisdiction, Adobe offers a single sovereign setup.2
  • Real-Time CDP and Customer Journey Analytics: you choose the region when Adobe provisions the platform, in North America, Europe or Asia Pacific, and Customer Journey Analytics keeps its data in that region. Data cannot be moved to another data center later.3, 4
  • Target: runs on central clusters in Oregon, Dublin and Singapore, and keeps each visitor's profile on the edge cluster closest to them.5
  • Marketo Engage: stored on AWS in a single region, with no replication to another region.6
  • Brand Visibility: keeps no personal data, and raw CDN logs are not kept after processing.7

Security certifications and encryption

  • AEM as a Cloud Service, which includes Edge Delivery Services, holds ISO 9001, ISO 27017, CSA STAR Level 2, IRAP, SOC 2, SOC 3 and SOC 2 + HIPAA.2
  • Edge Delivery Services uses TLS 1.2 or greater in transit and AES256 at rest.2 Experience Platform data, which Real-Time CDP and Customer Journey Analytics use, is encrypted in transit and at rest.8 Marketo Engage encrypts every instance at rest with AES 256-bit.9
  • We will attach Adobe's current certification list for each product in this proposal, from the Adobe Trust Center.

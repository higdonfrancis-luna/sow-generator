TWO RULES OF THUMB

Build decides the products; both pages decide the services. The Services step's library offers lines for the products on the canvas, plus custom lines.

A product with every line X'd stays on the canvas. The client may already own it, like CJA in the cross-sell play, so it stays in the picture with $0 of services.

Concept. Today's tool already keeps one list of services in the browser; the canvas would be a second view of that list.

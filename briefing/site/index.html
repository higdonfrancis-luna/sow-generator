No engineering team and no database server. Adam set the rules and approved every change; ChatGPT wrote the first draft and Claude Code wrote the rest, one numbered step at a time. Here are the four questions people ask, answered.

01 · The method

How was it built?

The same five-step loop, 96 times.

  1. An idea or a findingFrom Adam, a reviewer, a partner or an automated audit.
  2. A picture firstA mockup of the change before any code is written.
  3. Adam says yesNothing is built without an explicit go.
  4. A numbered stepA small script makes the change, so every step can be replayed.
  5. Checks, then publishClick-through tests must pass before anything goes live.

Adam never wrote code by hand. He wrote the rules, in plain English, and they still govern the tool: partners see the work and the hours, never Adobe software pricing; partners enter their own rate; every service links to the Adobe page it came from; nothing a partner types leaves their browser.

The AI did the typing, the research and the testing. Adam did the judgment: what to build, what a partner would believe, and what was not true.

02 · The data

What data does it use?

No database. Three knowledge files, built from Adobe’s own public pages.

catalog.jsonThe catalog

34 Adobe products, 9 outside systems (CDN, CRM, data warehouse and more), 151 connections, 531 services and the preset plays. Every service has low, typical and high hours.

rfp_facts.jsonThe facts

Hosting, security, privacy, sign-in and licensing answers, and what each product does, each backed by a quote from an Adobe page.

rfp_method.jsonThe methods

How each service gets delivered: 2,007 steps, 1,813 deliverables and 1,108 things to ask the client for.

Where it came from. AI research agents searched Adobe’s public documentation product by product. Every service was saved with the page it came from and a short quote, then a second pass re-checked the quotes. A service with no Adobe source is left out of the build.

723 Adobe source pagesExperience League 594Adobe Help 65aem.live 23Adobe Developer 21Product descriptions 15Trust Center 3Adobe Business 2

What came from people. The plays came from the Brand Visibility partner deck. The hours are planning estimates (Adobe publishes none), tuned by reviewers. The $100 to $300 an hour range comes from public 2026 partner rate surveys. There is no customer, account or pricing data in the tool.

Why files and not a database. It is private (it runs in the partner’s browser), free to host, works offline, and every change to the data is a reviewed GitHub commit, which is the audit trail.

IN THE PARTNER’S BROWSER

The canvas hands the play, size, rate and every service ID to the SOW editor. The RFI Builder reads an RFP, matches each question to products with 8,187 phrases, drafts answers from the facts and methods, and sends the chosen services into the same SOW by the same IDs.

04 · The refinement

How did it get to here?

A one-page draft on October 28. Reviewed, broken and fixed until November 13.

  1. OCT 28The first draft

    ChatGPT builds a one-page SOW tool: six templates, 28 services.

  2. OCT 29Adam’s rules, and live for partners

    Work and hours only, the partner’s own rate, a source on every service, a Small, Typical, Large switch. A teammate’s review adds the $100 to $300 rate range; a product marketing review adds outcomes and risks for each phase.

  3. OCT 30 TO 31The architecture canvas

    Partners pick products and the products connect themselves where Adobe documents a connection. The canvas becomes the front page.

  4. NOV 1 TO 5Hardening

    Outside reviews by ChatGPT, a 28-agent verification pass and a 45-finding audit. Every finding was reproduced, fixed and given a test.

  5. NOV 8 TO 9The RFI Builder

    Drop in a client’s RFP and get cited draft answers, then carry the services into the SOW. The site moves to Adobe Proto.

  6. NOV 10 TO 11From 14 products to 34

    Four waves of research added 20 products. In blind scoring against an existing Adobe draft of the same bank RFP, the RFI Builder’s answers were preferred on all 8 questions.

  7. NOV 13Today’s snapshot

    531 services, 723 Adobe sources, 35 of 35 core test suites passing, and a build that reproduces byte for byte.

Read the calculation and the counting rules

Volume

Hours released × share reinvested ÷ hours per new proposal, capped by the qualified opportunities currently left unsubmitted.

Conversion

Existing submissions × win-rate improvement, plus additional submissions × their expected win rate.

Adobe value

Additional expected wins × implementation realization × tool attribution × annual Adobe subscription value. Partner services fees are excluded.

Calendar-year revenue

Annual subscription value × an assumed in-year recognition fraction. Actual accounting, start dates and contracts must be supplied by Finance.

Definitions: Adobe ARR definition · Adobe revenue recognition policy.

Do not add partner labor capacity or pipeline value to Adobe ARR. Multiple partners pursuing one customer must be deduplicated; existing pipeline wins are not all incremental.

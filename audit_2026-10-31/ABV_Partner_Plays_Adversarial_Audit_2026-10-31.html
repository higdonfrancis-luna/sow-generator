Independent adversarial review · 31 October 2026

ABV partner plays:
credible thesis, unvalidated economics.

Prepared for Adam Higdon ahead of discussion with Deloitte and Adobe’s CTO.

Do not present the current tool as a validated commercial offering.

After correcting the material errors, present it as an offering-design prototype for joint technical, delivery and customer validation. The documentation supports substantial product capability. It does not establish partner profit, justified effort or customer return.

Required fixesPlay assessmentEconomicsValidation planReference audit

What was reviewed

The supplied abvsow-site-one-website.zip, containing the Build canvas and SOW editor; the live site’s initial page and standalone ABV services path; all 172 distinct citation URLs for retrieval; and risk-prioritized primary-source checks of the core plays and selected custom connections. The Build catalog contains 14 products, 59 connections and 184 service lines. The focused SOW catalog contains 40 entries.

The live ABV default matched the attachment’s $210,000 scenario. This was not a complete browser regression, an execution of the Adobe integrations, an audit of customer environments, or a legal/compliance certification. No verified partner delivery actuals, approved rate cards or customer purchase commitments were supplied in the package. The public site was not modified.

Strongest element

The original scoped services include deliverables, acceptance criteria and exclusions. Several correctly distinguish human review, manual workflows and non-guaranteed outcomes.

Greatest weakness

Product documentation and illustrative labor estimates are presented close enough together to imply that the former validates the latter.

Required fixes before the executive demonstration

These are substantive credibility and quoting issues, not cosmetic preferences.

Blocker · economics

Fees are presented as what a partner makes

The live heading “What a partner makes on this play” sits above hours multiplied by rates. There is no cost model, staffing mix, overrun provision, presales expense or margin calculation. The $100–$300 partner-rate statement has no provenance in the package and simply reuses the numerical range when the currency changes.

Fix: Rename the result Illustrative services fees. State that hours and scope are partner planning assumptions. Remove the unsupported market-rate range. Use the partner’s approved rate card and a private role-based cost/margin model.

Blocker · calculator

One additional prompt can double unrelated implementation hours

In the SOW editor, 200 prompts selects Small; 201 selects Typical. That switches EDS implementation from 480 to 960 hours and Concierge implementation from 320 to 640 hours, without changing their deliverables. The AEM play total changes from $267,600 to $484,800 at the default rate. The free-text parser also reads “200 prompts across 3 markets” as 2003 and selects Large.

Fix: Use a numeric prompt-count field and separate scope dimensions. Estimate monitoring by prompts, languages, engines and review cadence; site delivery by blocks, templates and integrations; migration by URLs and complexity; Concierge by sources, journeys, products and test requirements. Keep manual overrides with an explanation.

Blocker · scope

The optimization pilot contradicts its own citation

L03’s starting basis is up to 10 URLs; its scope is at least 20 eligible URLs and an Impact Measurement Engine report after two weeks. Adobe conditions measurement on eligible opportunities and at least 20 High Potential URLs. A report is not guaranteed for every optimization or URL, and the example timing is not a delivery SLA.

Fix: Agree one consistent quantity. Separate deployment/rollback acceptance from conditional impact measurement. Confirm opportunity eligibility and sufficient data before promising the report, particularly for Commerce PDP enrichment.

Evidence: Impact Measurement Engine eligibility

Blocker · technical claim

Visibility and bot traffic are not person-level revenue attribution

The canvas headline “AI visibility tied to revenue” is broader than the documented data path. ABV supplies aggregate bot activity by URL/host. Human AI-referral visits and conversions come from normal CJA web data; that human-referral analysis does not require the ABV dataset. C01 already excludes person-level linkage from an AI mention, which is a good safeguard.

Fix: Promote that boundary to the canvas. Use AI-agent activity and observed referral outcomes. Show bot activity, monitored visibility, observed human referral conversion and experimentally supported lift as different measures.

Evidence: ABV–CJA integration and data boundaries

Blocker · implementation

Fastly does not automatically mean CDN readiness is complete

C07 says Commerce cloud infrastructure with Fastly does not need the readiness service. The cited documentation singles out AEM Cloud Service managed Fastly for self-service and provides a separate Fastly BYOCDN configuration route. The blanket Commerce exception is not established.

Fix: Rename the service CDN readiness and routing where required. Qualify the real topology, existing routing, owner, cache separation and rollback. Exclude work only after readiness is verified.

Evidence: Fastly BYOCDN implementation

Blocker · architecture

The canvas mixes native integrations with partner-managed workflows

ABV→Brand Concierge is a manually designed question-to-source and content workflow in C04, which expressly excludes a custom/native ABV connector. The canvas still places it under “How the data flows,” while the chooser says products connect where Adobe documents a connection. EDS claims also blur AI-only CDN changes with permanent source-content publishing.

Fix: Label every connection by type: native integration, shared-platform data path, supported extension, or partner-designed workflow. Show prerequisites and availability beside each arrow. For the FSI edge, use Partner-managed question-to-approved-source workflow; no native connector assumed. Qualify the actual EDS authoring/CDN route.

Evidence: AEM managed-CDN routing

Blocker · availability

Concierge profile personalization needs explicit qualification

The custom RT-CDP→Concierge canvas presents profile lookup as normal functionality. Adobe’s release notes mark that integration Limited Availability; the FAQ still describes anonymous scope and future profile/journey lookup. These official sources conflict. The detailed XA09 scope is more cautious than the canvas.

Fix: Use the anonymous public-information experience as the baseline. Gate profile-based capabilities on written product-team confirmation for the specific tenant, use case and region. Do not use a broad architecture blueprint to override release status.

Evidence: Brand Concierge FAQ · Brand Concierge release notes

Blocker · custom configurator

Supported activity types and entitlements are not reliably enforced

“Every Target test” is incorrect: Auto-Target and Automated Personalization do not support CJA as the reporting source. Separately, the tool requires RT-CDP for one CJA→Target activation path although Adobe now permits Target-destination entitlement through RT-CDP or AJO. EDS forms connections can also require Forms and Fusion capabilities not represented as required selections.

Fix: Limit the claim to supported Target activities, validate reporting configuration before launch, model alternative entitlements, and require external prerequisites before marking a connection ready. Two selected product boxes are not sufficient evidence of deliverability.

Evidence: Target reporting in CJA: supported activity types · Target destination eligibility · Adaptive Forms prerequisites

High · SOW quality

The expanded catalog lacks the original SOW’s specificity

The 146 research-origin canvas entries have product-related scopes and evidence, but lack tailored deliverables, acceptance criteria, assumptions and exclusions. The exporter supplies generic text. The result looks more contract-ready than the service design actually is.

Fix: Mark these entries service concept, not quote-ready. Require quantities, prerequisites, a concrete deliverable and a testable acceptance criterion before export. Demonstrate a narrow reviewed set at the executive meeting.

High · scope integrity

New builds, existing estates and reusable work are mixed

FSI Scope describes an existing Concierge deployment while default F02 adds 640 hours for a new one. Custom Coworker+CJA selects both generic and specialized two-Skill packages at 56 hours each, although the copy says specialized work replaces equivalent generic work. Four distinct Skills could be justified, but the tool does not make that distinction.

Fix: Ask new / existing healthy / remediation required for each application. Track unique workflows and reusable setup. Enforce overlap rules or require an explicit distinct scope before both lines are charged.

High · commercial terms

“Year one” and delay treatment can misstate economics

The canvas always adds implementation plus 12 monthly periods. If operations begin after implementation, that is not necessarily revenue delivered in the first 12 months. The generated SOW also says delays extend the timeline, not the fee, without a capacity, pause or restart policy.

Fix: Show actual service start/end months or label the total implementation plus 12-month operating term. Add the partner’s billing model, review windows, dependency deadlines, pause/restart terms and change-control reference. Complete these under the partner’s MSA process.

High · FSI operating model

Approved source material does not approve every generated answer

FSI’s detailed scope properly leaves compliance decisions to the customer and avoids certification. Preserve that. But sampled launch approval and an optional monthly review do not establish a complete operating model.

Fix: Require a named operating owner, current source versions, golden-answer tests, refusal/escalation scenarios, failure handling, source-change triggers and controlled rollout. The work may be customer-owned rather than partner-billed. Qualify the named handoff platform; Adobe’s documented live-chat/booking route uses Marketo Qualifier, and its live-chat retention is 30 days.

Evidence: Brand Concierge pre-go-live checks · Brand Concierge handoff prerequisites

Fix before demo · coherence

Irrelevant ABV text leaks into other SOWs

Coworker and custom non-ABV documents inherit ABV CDN-forwarding dependencies and prompt-oriented launch outcomes.

Fix: Generate dependencies and stage outcomes from the selected products/services. Verify exported output, not only the canvas.

The recurring-service thesis carries most of the financial weight

Standalone ABV’s recurring program contributes $187,200, or 89.1% of the displayed total. It budgets 40 hours/month for portfolio review and 64 hours/month for four page refreshes and one new page, with two review rounds. That may be justified by difficult enterprise work. The package does not yet establish that it is necessary, wanted or repeatable at that price.

Adobe’s edge-optimization documentation describes fast no-code deployment after onboarding. A CTO can reasonably ask what the partner does that the product and the existing team do not. Sell the missing expertise: source truth, content judgment, approvals, measurement design, hard integration work, organizational adoption and accountable operations. Show how automation improves delivery efficiency.

Evidence: Optimize at Edge onboarding and automation

The transformation total is not all incremental ABV opportunity

The AEM play contains 1,600 hours for discovery, site implementation, migration, launch and governance. At the default rate that is $240,000, 49.5% of its displayed total. A customer may already have a separately funded website program. Separate total program services, ABV-attributable incremental scope and work already contracted.

Two transparent stress tests

Partner delivery margin

Assume, solely for illustration, a $90 fully loaded delivery cost/hour. At $15,600 fees and 104 actual hours, contribution before other expenses is $6,240, or 40%. If a fixed-fee commitment requires 130 hours, it falls to $3,900, or 25%. A higher billing rate alone does not prove a profitable delivery model.

Customer break-even

Assume a 40% contribution margin on truly incremental customer revenue. Covering the $210,000 service fee alone requires $525,000 of incremental revenue. Software and customer internal cost raise the hurdle. Validated cost savings can contribute separately. This is an illustrative hurdle calculation, not a forecast.

Replace arbitrary bands with documented estimating inputs

For each service, record unit quantity, complexity, existing maturity, roles, hours per role, assumptions, exclusions, estimate owner/date and confidence. Use independent partner estimates before exposing the current numbers to avoid anchoring. Calibrate against actual pilot delivery. A low/base/high range should describe concrete scope or uncertainty, not an unsupported small/typical/large label.

Coworker’s default maintenance is 384 hours/year for two Skills and one team, versus 56 hours for initial Skill construction. Adoption may justify substantial effort, but recurring maintenance should depend on actual usage, update volume and demonstrated value. Start with a bounded adoption program, then choose the operating tier.

Measurement design: Keep a stable prompt cohort for trend reporting and a separate discovery cohort. Record market, language, engine, configuration and changes; report sampling limitations. Distinguish citation/mention movement, bot accessibility, observed referrals, conversion quality and business impact. A practical path to a defensible offer

  1. Correct the demo now. Fix the false/overbroad claims, URL quantity contradiction, Fastly exception, sizing/parser issue, entitlement rules, duplicate scope and irrelevant document text. Label all fees as illustrative. Restrict the demo to reviewed services.
  2. Ask Adobe for a capability review. For each demonstrated connection, have the product/solution owner confirm availability, supported architecture, prerequisites and responsibilities. Record contradictions as open questions until resolved.
  3. Ask Deloitte for independent delivery estimates. Provide two realistic client briefs, their estate, maturity, scope units and acceptance criteria. Have delivery leads estimate roles and effort before showing the prototype’s numbers. Reconcile differences and record ownership.
  4. Validate buying intent. Identify buyer, budget, trigger, current agency/internal responsibility, competing solution and reason to buy now. Separate new spend from replacement or expansion of existing work.
  5. Run two or three bounded paid pilots. A proposed 6–8 week window is a planning hypothesis, subject to readiness and measurement feasibility. Track actual effort, rework, approval delays, costs, customer outcomes and reusable assets. Define expand/stop criteria before starting.
  6. Earn the recurring program. Expand only with an agreed backlog, operating owner, measurable benefit and renewal criteria. Offer execution, governance and enablement tiers tied to actual needs, not automatic twelve-month labor assumptions.

Five questions the executives should be invited to challenge

  • Which capabilities and prerequisites does Adobe confirm for these exact client scenarios?
  • Which tasks would a customer buy from Deloitte rather than perform in the product?
  • What unique delivery expertise or reusable IP makes the partner valuable and profitable?
  • Which hours survive an independent estimate and a real delivery?
  • What evidence makes the customer renew after the pilot?

Suggested positioning after the corrections

This is a partner services design prototype grounded in Adobe’s documented capabilities. The scopes, effort bands and fees are planning assumptions for joint validation. We want Adobe to confirm the supported architecture and Deloitte to validate delivery economics, then test the offers with real customers before standardizing them.

Readiness decision: An internal review of the weaknesses can happen now. An executive offering-design workshop is reasonable after the blockers are corrected and assumptions are visible. A validated rate card, customer-ready SOW or proven partner-profit claim is not supported yet.

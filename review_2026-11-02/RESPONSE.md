## Smaller corrections

- **Money in cents.** Each line's fee rounds half up to the cent; totals add the rounded fees; the term fee of a monthly line is its rounded monthly fee times the months. The CSV, the cards, the SOW and the canvas show the same figures, with cents only when there are any (the SOW used to show £49,175.1).
- **L03 Last-Modified.** The deployment pilot's scope checks that each selected page sends a Last-Modified header where the opportunity needs one; the assumption says what Adobe does (it pauses the optimization for review when the page changes and skips it without the header) and that a page without it is replaced or the customer adds the header. The pilot deploys "using the agreed Optimize at Edge route" instead of configuring one, so routing has one owner: already in place, self-serve in Brand Visibility for AEM as a Cloud Service sites on Adobe's managed Fastly CDN (set up in the pilot), the customer's CDN team, or CDN readiness and routing in Commerce. Selected URLs sit inside the routed paths.
- **C03.** The sentence "Adobe documents no direct publishing link from Brand Visibility into AEM" is gone. Adobe's Brand Visibility product page returned an error on November 2, so it couldn't be checked; the text now neither claims nor rules out an Adobe connection: before work begins, the authoring route and how Brand Visibility's findings reach it are confirmed, through an Adobe connection only where Adobe confirms one is enabled for the customer, otherwise through this partner-designed workflow; Optimize at Edge changes only what AI agents receive at the CDN, and publishing stays with the customer's review and approval.
- **F02 references.** The card cites Adobe's pre-go-live checklist and rollout plan and the live chat and meeting booking page, each with its own title. Its assumptions give Marketo Qualifier's prerequisites in full (representatives as users in the Admin Console; a connected calendar for meeting booking, live chat availability for live chat) and say when Marketo Engage is needed: only for custom routing rules, which pick a team by Marketo attributes, and for writing concierge activities to the Marketo person record; round robin and direct-agent routing work without it. The golden-answer set is scored at the agreed level for correctness, helpfulness and brand fit, as Adobe's checklist scores it.
- **CW07 plugin package.** Typical names its quantity (the agreed Skills and up to two connections); Large's deliverable reads for one plugin or two; installing a plugin grants no access the users don't already have (Adobe's "Manage Plugins and Marketplaces", now its second reference).

# Step 46: the client sweep (Nov 4 2026)

Adam: "we should always say your client... we are building this for Accenture and Deloitte and other systems integrators and they have clients", then "yes" to the sweep.

Rule: in the statement of work the party is **the Client** (a defined party, as the partner's SOWs name it); where the tool speaks to the partner it says **your client**; a client's own customers stay **customers**, and Adobe's product and feature names (Customer Journey Analytics, Real-Time Customer Profile, Customer Attributes) stay as Adobe writes them.

How it was decided: every occurrence was listed with its context (`sweep46_extract.py`, 337 wordings in 348 places, leaving out the earlier-wording records saved engagements are matched against, Adobe's quotes, page titles and the problem box's match keywords); five classifiers decided each one and five more checked every decision; Claude then read all of them, corrected four (a client's own CDN is "your client's CDN") found two the listing had missed (a sentence ending in "the customer:", and the default responsibilities) and made "Run the customer pilot" read "Run the Client's pilot". Applied by `sweep46_apply.py` and `patch_step46.py`.

Saved work is safe: a saved service still in the earlier wording takes the new wording (PRIOR46, 41 services), and text a partner edited stays exactly as written; the same goes for the FSI play's objective and the default responsibilities. `node sow/test_step46.js` checks all of it.

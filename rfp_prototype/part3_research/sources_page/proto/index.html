How it stays honest

  1. Every sentence lists the exact words it relies on from its Adobe page. A checker looks for those words on the page and stops the tool from being built if any are missing.
  2. Where Adobe's own pages disagree, such as where Brand Concierge data is hosted, the fact goes to the partner as "Confirm with Adobe", never to the client.
  3. When no Adobe page covers a product on a topic, the tool drafts nothing for it and tells the partner to ask Adobe.
  4. The facts were checked against Adobe's pages on November 9, 2026. Adobe updates its pages often, and the date shows how fresh the facts are.

## Method and limits

Every scored requirement in the four committed Round 3 panels is included: 55 requirements, each judged through four lenses. Means below are recomputed from raw judge scores and panel keys, not rounded summaries. The exact scores, judge reasons, source references, questions, causes and fixes are retained in [ROUND4_DIAGNOSIS.json](ROUND4_DIAGNOSIS.json).

For a scored requirement, gap = mean AI score minus mean Round 3 no-AI score. We allocate each positive gap among observed causes using explicit analyst shares that sum to one. A cause cost is the sum of gap multiplied by its share. This is a transparent prioritization estimate, not a causal experiment or a prediction of the gain from a fix. Negative gaps remain visible. Missing drafts receive no invented quality score and are counted separately.

**Live-page reproduction is complete.** [round4_baseline_trace.json](round4_baseline_trace.json) records all four permitted RFIs in the built baseline page: classification, catalog product/service match, `kbPlan`, raw `method()` sections, fitted `written()` sections and every exact removed line. There were zero page errors and no missing expected labels. All 56 keyed method outputs match their saved Round 3 text byte for byte; all 18 missing drafts were reproduced. Source and built-page hashes stayed unchanged during capture. Per-question evidence is also joined into the aggregate JSON.

 1. About Cedar Prairie Mutual 
 Cedar Prairie Mutual Insurance Company is a regional mutual insurer founded in 1921 and based in Cedar Rapids, Iowa. We write auto, home, farm and life coverage in Iowa, Illinois, Minnesota, Nebraska and Wisconsin, with about 410,000 policyholders served through 1,150 independent agents and a contact center of 140 people. Our public website, cedarprairie-example.com, has about 1.1 million visits a month. It runs on Drupal 9, which reaches the end of its support, and our marketing team needs a developer for most changes. More of our prospects now research coverage through AI assistants before they visit us, and we do not know how those assistants describe us. 
 2. Scope of work 
 Cedar Prairie has selected Adobe Experience Manager Sites with Edge Delivery Services, Adobe Brand Visibility, Adobe Brand Concierge, Customer Journey Analytics and Adobe Coworker. None of them is set up yet. We are looking for one implementation partner to rebuild and migrate our website, implement the five products, connect them to our systems, train our people and provide monthly services after launch. Our systems today: Drupal 9 (website), Akamai (CDN), Salesforce Financial Services Cloud (agents), Snowflake (policy and claims data), Microsoft Power BI (executive reporting), Marketo Engage (email), Genesys Cloud (contact center) and Okta (employee sign-on). We expect the new website and Brand Concierge to launch within ten months of contract signing, with Customer Journey Analytics reporting live within twelve months.

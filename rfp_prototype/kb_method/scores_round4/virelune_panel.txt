THE RFP (as the client issued it)
VIRELUNE UNIVERSITY
Request for information: public website and applicant paperwork renewal
Issued 13 November 2026
Background and scope
Virelune University is an independently governed university with 18,400 students, three campuses and recruitment offices serving the UK, Ireland and Spain. We are seeking information from firms that could rebuild and operate our public recruitment website and a small set of applicant paperwork services. This RFI is for planning and shortlisting; it is not an instruction to begin delivery.
Our public estate runs on Drupal 10 and contains approximately 6,800 HTML pages and 9,200 downloadable documents across one main domain and four faculty subdomains. There are 78 occasional editors and 22 site owners. About 900 pages have Spanish equivalents; the rest are English only. Peak traffic reaches 1.2 million visits per month during application periods. We plan to retire at least a quarter of the existing pages after owners review them. Historic committee papers will remain in a separately hosted archive.
Our CRM is Microsoft Dynamics 365, our warehouse is Snowflake, our reporting tool is Power BI, our CDN is Cloudflare, and our contact centre uses Genesys Cloud CX. Staff use Microsoft Entra ID. These systems will remain. A separate student records system is outside this project; the CRM will remain the source for application identifiers and applicant contact details supplied to the new services.
We are evaluating AEM Sites, AEM Forms, Acrobat Sign and Target. Explain where our intended use would require a different configuration, additional entitlement or a change to our assumptions. We have not yet agreed a license package. We will not license a customer data platform, a marketing automation suite, a commerce platform or a separate digital asset management product through this programme. Paid advertising, learning systems, tuition payments, student portals and replacement of the contact centre are excluded.
The proposed first release covers the public website, 12 enquiry or request forms and three document-signing processes: an offer-acceptance acknowledgement, a placement participation agreement and a scholarship acceptance form. Legal and student services staff will approve the wording and decide which processes are suitable for electronic signature. No passport scans, medical information or payment card details should be collected through the new forms. Target testing is intended only for public recruitment pages, not signed-in applicant services or decisions about admission.
We expect mobilisation in January 2027, approval of design and scope by March, a faculty pilot in June and main-site launch by 16 August 2027. Include the first six months of support in your response. University staff will provide policy decisions, source content and subject-matter review. The supplier should identify the client effort and dependencies needed to make those dates credible.

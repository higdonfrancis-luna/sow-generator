2.2 How would you connect our Customer Journey Analytics data to AI assistant traffic, and what do you need from us?

Draft, not yet reviewed

Our approach

  • Brand Visibility sends aggregate AI-agent activity (from CDN logs, by URL and host) into CJA through an Experience Platform source. AI-referred visits and conversions come from CJA's own web data, and Brand Visibility reads those results for its business impact view.
  • Four separate measures. AI-agent activity (from CDN logs) and monitored visibility (tracked prompts) are what Brand Visibility adds; AI-referred visits and conversions come from your CJA web data, which ordinary referrer reporting can show without it; lift comes only from a test. The data is aggregate: nothing ties a person to an AI mention.

What we would do

  • We will configure the supported ABV bot-data connection and map URLs and hosts; identify AI-referred visits in the existing CJA web data with a derived field on AI referring domains; and align observed AI-referral and conversion measures with existing CJA data.
  • We will define up to five audiences from AI-referred visits and their outcomes in the CJA data view, publish them to Real-Time Customer Profile, and agree with you how each audience is used in its existing channels.
  • We will plan the upgrade path, implement Web SDK alongside the Analytics source connector for history, port reporting API use and exports, and migrate projects and components.

Our approach

  • Forms built with AEM Forms in the Universal Editor for Edge Delivery pages can use the Submit to Marketo Engage action; submissions run through AEM Publish, and AEM Forms needs its own license.
  • Lead forms built with AEM Forms on Edge Delivery pages submit to Marketo Engage through AEM Publish, ready for scoring and nurture.

What we would do

  • We will build the lead forms in Universal Editor, configure the Submit to Marketo Engage action with field mapping to Marketo, and test that submissions create or update leads.
  • We will train the marketing users on programs, smart campaigns and templates, and document the new instance setup so the team can run it after launch.
  • We will run the go-live checklist, cut over DNS and the CDN, verify Brand Visibility, analytics, redirects and forms on the live site, and support your team for two weeks after launch.

After launch

2.8 Which Adobe products would you use to personalize the site for known customers, and how would results be measured?

Draft, not yet reviewed

Our approach

  • A Target activity that uses CJA as its reporting source sends its data to Experience Platform (Web SDK is the preferred method), Adobe creates the Adobe Target Classification Events lookup dataset, and activity reports are built in CJA.
  • Supported Target activities report in CJA with its full journey data; Auto-Target and Automated Personalization do not. With Real-Time CDP or Journey Optimizer, audiences built in CJA reach Target too.

What we would do

  • We will add the event dataset and the Adobe Target Classification Events lookup dataset (created by Adobe) to a CJA connection, then add activity and experience dimensions with persistence and the Experimentation context labels. Before each launch, confirm the activity type is supported and its reporting source is CJA with the agreed data view, since it can't change once live.
  • We will build journey audiences in CJA, publish them to Experience Platform, and share them with Target through the Adobe Target destination, which needs Real-Time CDP or Journey Optimizer and a Target-enabled datastream, for the agreed personalization activities.
  • We will build Experimentation panel reports and a reusable Workspace template for Target activities, showing lift and confidence on your CJA success metrics.

  • Experience League: Adobe Target connection

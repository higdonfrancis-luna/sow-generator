1. About Tallgrass Home & Garden

Tallgrass Home & Garden is a home improvement and garden retailer with 86 stores in nine Midwestern states and an online store. Our website serves about 2.4 million visits a month. It runs on a content management system we built ourselves twelve years ago, and our marketing team needs a developer for almost every change.

We have chosen Adobe Experience Manager Sites as a Cloud Service and Customer Journey Analytics, and neither is set up yet. We are looking for one implementation partner to stand up both, move our content, and leave our team able to run them.

2. Scope of work

The selected partner will implement AEM Sites for tallgrass-example.com, migrate about 1,200 pages from our current content management system, and implement Customer Journey Analytics so that our web, store and loyalty data can be analyzed together. Our store and loyalty data is in a Snowflake data warehouse, and our executives read their reports in Power BI. We expect the new site to launch within nine months of contract signing.

3. Questions for bidders

Answer each question in order. Keep each answer under 250 words unless the question asks for more.

Section A. AEM Sites

A.1 Describe how you would set up the AEM Sites Cloud Manager program, environments, deployment pipelines and user access.

A.2 Describe how you would build our site in AEM Sites, including the components and page templates our marketing team will use.

A.3 Describe how you would migrate about 1,200 pages of content from our current content management system into AEM Sites.

A.4 Describe your go-live and cutover approach for the new AEM Sites website, including how you protect our search rankings.

A.5 Describe how you would train our content authors to create and publish pages in AEM Sites without developer help.

A.6 Where is AEM Sites as a Cloud Service hosted, and which security certifications does it hold?

Section B. Customer Journey Analytics

B.1 Describe your approach to Customer Journey Analytics data preparation for our web, store and loyalty data.

B.2 Describe how you would set up Customer Journey Analytics connections and data views, and how you would stitch web and store activity for the same customer.

B.3 Describe how you would bring store and loyalty data from our Snowflake data warehouse into Customer Journey Analytics.

B.4 Describe the Customer Journey Analytics projects and dashboards you would build for our marketing and merchandising teams.

B.5 Describe how our executives could read Customer Journey Analytics data in Power BI.

B.6 Describe how you would onboard and train our analysts on Customer Journey Analytics.

Section C. Working together and after launch

C.1 Describe how page views and clicks on the new AEM Sites website would be collected for Customer Journey Analytics.

C.2 Describe the monthly services you would provide for AEM Sites after launch.

C.3 Describe the monthly analysis and insights service you would provide for Customer Journey Analytics after launch.

Section D. Your firm

D.1 Provide a brief overview of your firm, including years in business and your Adobe partner level.

D.2 Provide three references from retail clients for similar AEM Sites or Customer Journey Analytics work.

D.3 Describe your proposed project team, including each person's role and relevant experience.

D.4 Describe your project management approach and how you report progress.

D.5 Provide your pricing for the scope of work, with hours and rates by role.

4. Evaluation

Proposals are due as one PDF to the contact above by the due date. Proposals will be scored on the approach to Sections A to C (50 percent), the firm's experience and team (30 percent) and price (20 percent). Tallgrass Home & Garden may hold interviews with up to three bidders in January 2027.

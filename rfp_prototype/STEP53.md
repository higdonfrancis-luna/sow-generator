## The connection cards: a client-voice version of how and gain

A connection card's how and gain were written for the partner: they say "your client", keep Adobe's lifecycle labels (Legacy, maintenance mode, co-innovation, "not currently offered to health care, finance") and tell the partner what to confirm or prove. The drafted answer ran that text through a pronoun swap and sent it to the client. Now each card can carry a client-voice version, `sowHow` and `sowGain`, that the draft prefers, and a `sowNote` holding the cautions the client version leaves out, which the partner reads in the note: under Confirm with Adobe when it is about Adobe, otherwise as a line of its own ("Not in the client's text: ..."). A ticked ask reaches the client in the SOW's own client wording (the connection's `sowPrereqs`, matched to the partner's prerequisite by its words, since the two lists are not always in the same order), so no lifecycle word rides into "What we would need from you" either. They live in `sow/product_copy_rules.json` as `connection_client` {id: {how, gain, note}} and `sow/product_copy.py` writes them into `sow/canvas/catalog.json` at build, the same way the SOW's own client wording for availability and prerequisites is applied. The canvas card itself is unchanged: the partner still reads Adobe's labels there.

A sweep of all 75 cards through the voice rewrite found 29 whose client text carried a label or a caution (the review named ten); Brand Visibility + CJA (finding 5.4) made 30, and a round of skeptics on the built page found six more whose card text told the partner what to do on the canvas or how a line is priced (Real-Time CDP + Brand Concierge, AEM Sites + Edge Delivery, Commerce + Journey Optimizer, Commerce + CJA, Workfront + Edge Delivery, Cloud storage + CJA), 36 in all. The wording was drafted by helpers working in batches of three cards, each batch checked twice (facts kept, voice and rules) and sent back once where a checker found a problem; three items were still open after the second pass and were fixed by hand (a hedge on Brand Concierge + CJA, a long first sentence on Real-Time CDP + Edge Delivery, a missing comma on Commerce + AEM Assets), and Coworker + Workfront was set to the SOW's own client sentence, as in Adam's picture: "Adobe confirms that Coworker is available to your organization before this service starts." The rule for the client text: the partner speaks as "we" to "you"; every product fact, route, license and measured number of the original is kept; where Adobe documents no route or marks one Legacy, the text states what Adobe documents and that we confirm the route with Adobe before the work starts, without the label; the removed caution goes into the note. Journey Optimizer + Brand Concierge has no services behind it, so it drafts nothing for the client and the No match panel tells the partner what Adobe says.

## The sentence builder and the voice rewrite (finding 5.3)

Thirty of 201 service lines came out broken ("We will where ...", "We will for ...", "We will the agreed dashboards"). `doLine` now keeps a leading setting clause and puts "we will" where the work starts, cutting at the first comma or colon that is followed by a verb ("For the agreed file feeds in Amazon S3, Azure Blob Storage, Azure Data Lake Storage Gen2, Google Cloud Storage or SFTP, we will create ..."; "Once Adobe confirms in writing that the integration is enabled for this tenant, use case and region, we will choose ..."), and every later sentence and semicolon clause that is an instruction becomes a promise too ("...; for Cloud Manager standard repositories, we will configure branch delivery where enabled. We will confirm opportunity-specific enablement, then agree developer review, testing and release."), so the client never reads a bare instruction. The voice rewrite (`rfp`) turns the partner as an object into "us" ("a handoff managed by us", "gives us access", "between Adobe, you and us") and as a subject into "we" ("You provide the list and we map it"), partner compounds into "our" ("Our built and tested Skills"), "you and your client" and "the Client and partner" into "we and you" (finding 5.6), "Client-provided" into "your", "Client authorization" into "your authorization", "Client-authorized outreach actions" into "outreach actions you authorize", "a partner test method" into "our test method", "for the customer" into "for you", and conjugates does, authorizes, authors, requires and the other verbs the catalog uses, including after an adverb ("you already export") and in a comma chain ("You own the videos, provide channel access and supply ..."); the conjugation rules apply only where we or you is the subject of the clause, so "A Skill we build summarizes ... and flags answers" keeps its verbs. A sweep of every line is in `test_rfp_step53.js`.

## What the client never reads, whatever the source (`clientScrub`)

Everything the response prints (the approach, every service line, every ticked prerequisite, every source title) also passes through `clientScrub`: the SOW's own client rules (`rules.sow_replacements` on the page: the Coworker industry sentence, co-innovation, maintenance mode, "a limited number of customers", "in its current scope"), then the RFP's own swaps ("Legacy at.js route" becomes "at.js route (Adobe's earlier library)", "(the V2 card is Legacy)" goes, "legacy or enhanced connector" becomes "earlier or enhanced connector", "(legacy reference page)" goes from a source title, "on the canvas" becomes "in this proposal"), then any leftover "legacy" becomes "earlier", "where the SOW identifies" becomes "where there is", "the SOW" becomes "the statement of work", "Do not assume" becomes "We do not assume" and "state whether" becomes "agree with you whether", and a sentence written for the partner about the canvas ("on the AEM Sites card", "this card", "under More services", "service out", "priced once", "counted once") is dropped. Only those canvas phrases drop a sentence: "product cards and side-by-side comparisons" and "the destination card" are the client's own words and stay (the first build dropped any sentence with "card", which left Brand Concierge + Commerce reading "We will cart, checkout and order history are outside the documented scope"). A ticked prerequisite whose SOW wording says "is confirmed with Adobe" is sorted under Confirm with Adobe rather than sent to the client. Two named products whose only connection has no services behind it (Journey Optimizer + Brand Concierge) draft nothing, even when a line's name matches a word in the question; the No match panel tells the partner what Adobe says, also when the pair is reached through the Drawn from chips.

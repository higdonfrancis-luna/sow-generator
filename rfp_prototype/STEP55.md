## What the partner sees

- **Approve and Leave out are toggles.** Under every drafted answer: Approve, Edit, Leave out, Next to review. A click on Approve marks the row "Approved" and the button reads "Approved, undo"; a second click takes it back. Leave out marks the row "Left out" and reads "Left out, undo" the same way. A firm question has Approve, Leave out and Next to review under its box; Approve needs text ("Type the answer first." otherwise), and emptying an approved answer takes the approval away. The capability box and a product this catalog does not cover have the same two buttons.
- **The downloads carry approved answers only.** The client copy (Download RFP response) prints an approved answer in full and "Your answer:" with a blank for every other question; a question left out is always blank. The summary bar has a checkbox, "Include the answers not yet approved in the downloads, marked as drafts": ticked, a draft goes into the client copy under a "Draft, not yet approved" tag, into the Excel's Response cell with the prefix "Draft, not yet approved: " and into the write-back the same way. The Excel's Status column says Approved, Draft (when included), Draft, not included, Not answered (left out), Still to write or Still to sort. The box is kept with the RFP in this tab.
- **To review.** The filters read All (14), Adobe products (8), Your firm (6), To sort (0), To review (14), Not picked up (3), as the picture shows, each count live. To review holds the Adobe and firm questions with no decision yet and shrinks with every Approve or Leave out.
- **Next to review** walks the rows the current filter shows, firm questions included, to the next one without a decision, after the open row; it never lands on the open row itself, and when nothing else is left it says so in a toast ("Every other question is reviewed.", "Everything else this filter shows is reviewed."). On load the first Adobe answer still opens by itself (Nov 9).
- **Start over** replaces Clear, at the right of the filters, and asks first in the tool's own modal style: "Start over? This clears the RFP, its questions and every answer, tick and note on this tab. Save my work first if you want to keep them." with Cancel and "Clear the RFP". Confirmed, it clears the list, the answer panel, the summary bar and this tab's storage.
- **Rows open from the keyboard.** Every row is a button (role, tabindex); Enter or Space opens it; a focused row shows a red outline. The pile menu inside keeps its own keys.
- **A pile change follows through.** Moving the open row to another pile re-renders the panel at once (the firm box, or the draft drawn again) and clears the row's draft, decision, answer, notes, ticks, product picks and service ticks together, so nothing survives half-way.
- **The pile shows twice** on a row, as a tag at the left and the menu at the right, because that is the row Adam approved in the decision 3 picture (finding 7.8 proposed showing it once; the picture wins).
- The response is titled from the client's file since step 53.
- **A question the catalog cannot match** (No match) has a box of its own with Approve and Leave out, like the capability box, so every row in To review can be resolved; an empty filter says so ("Nothing to review: every question has a decision."), and Next to review on an empty filter says so too.

## What the skeptics found, and what changed

Twenty skeptics (two per finding) drove the first build of steps 55 and 56. What they found is closed: restored approvals (after a reload, Open draft or Open my work) were not counted, previewed or handed off until each row was opened (now every approved answer's draft is drawn when the count needs it); a second write-back kept the cells an earlier one had filled, so drafts and withdrawn approvals reached the client's workbook (now the workbook's own cell values are kept from the first write and every cell is rewritten each time); a note typed under an ask was not stored until the next redraw (now at once); changing the products behind an approved answer kept the approval on text nobody had read (now the approval goes with the old text); a no-match row sat in To review with nothing to approve or leave out; Next to review restarted at the top when the filter hid the open row, and was silent on an empty filter; emptying an approved firm answer left the panel saying Approved; Escape did not close the confirmations; opening a row from the keyboard dropped the focus to the page; the intro still said "Adobe services"; "skipped" survived next to Left out, and Edit said "Save and use"; the tool's own Start over, which says it clears every step, left the RFP in place (now it goes too); a new drop or paste replaced an RFP with decisions on it without asking (now the same RFP dropped again, the way the client's workbook comes back after a reload, keeps every decision, and a different one asks first), and so did Open draft (now it asks too, and says nothing when the tool refused the file); Open my work had no button when nothing was on screen, rewrote approved rows' ticks and notes while saying nothing was merged, dropped a question the other side had made from Not picked up, took a priority field from the file as raw HTML, and accepted an empty file (now rows with a decision are never changed, the toast counts decisions, ticks and notes and added questions, rows from a file are checked field by field, and an empty file is refused); the preview counted an answer whose services were all unticked, promised to add services already on Your own play, and did not say when a named play gives way to Your own play; the leave warning fired after a tick was undone; an untick on an ask the redrafted answer no longer showed still dropped its prerequisite from the statement of work, and an unticked package ask left its "Assumes your client has" line in; a huge RFP overflowed the tab's storage silently (now the status line says to use Save my work).

Left as built, and why: To review counts firm questions and a sixth filter, Not picked up, stands beside the picture's five (steps 50 and 55 built them on purpose); the pile tag and menu both show (the approved picture); a service unticked on one answer still hands off when another approved answer carries it, and the hand-off turns a service back on that the partner switched off in the tool while an approved answer still ticks it (the RFP's ticks are the record); ticks and notes changed after approval change the approved text in place while the row stays Approved (the partner approves the answer, not a snapshot; they can undo); an unsaved Edit is not kept across a reload; a pile change wipes the row's work without a confirmation (the row says so by design, and a change back needs the text typed again); the response title turns the file name's underscores and hyphens into spaces (step 53).

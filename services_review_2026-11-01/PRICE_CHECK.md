## The three to check first

1. **The monthly Brand Visibility program** (O01 + O02): $15,600 a month at Typical, $187,200 a year. Your deck's run and operate is $3K to $10K a month. The monthly review alone (O01, $6,000 a month) is inside the deck's range; the content line (O02, $9,600 a month) is the difference. First Page Sage's compiled quotes put a median AI-search retainer at $6,850 a month and its upper mid-market band at $11,000 to $22,000. WebFX charges $12,000 a month for 250 prompts and 15 content assets, its own software included.
2. **The AEM web program** (A04, A01, A02, A03, A05): $240,000 at Typical. Your deck uses $200K. One partner advertises AEM Sites launches at $60,000 to $150,000, without counts.
3. **The Brand Concierge build** (F02): $96,000 at Typical, close to your deck's $100K. One partner advertises a simple launch from $14,000, and Adobe's go-live guide says Adobe's team configures the concierge from the customer's inputs. A regulated bank or insurer launch is bigger than a simple one, but this is the widest gap to a public price.

Also against your deck: Coworker on its own is $23,400 setup and $4,800 a month at Typical, against your deck's $40K setup and $5K a month. Most of its $81,000 is 12 months of adoption (CW06, $57,600); ChatGPT would keep adoption to 60 to 90 days.

## Is $150 an hour sensible?

Yes, as a comparison point, and on the low side. Partners enter their own approved rate. Published rates for this work run about $119 to $325 an hour: NextRow's Adobe support packages work out to $119 to $225 per included hour (mostly offshore delivery), and First Page Sage reports AI-search consulting at $125 to $325 (median $205). Promethean's 2026 survey of digital agencies in general found 29% charge $175 to $199. At $205 an hour every total in this note rises by about a third, and the gaps to your deck widen. Large firms' role rates vary widely: Deloitte's federal schedule lists $218 to $464 from senior associate to partner, for unrelated office, records and litigation work.

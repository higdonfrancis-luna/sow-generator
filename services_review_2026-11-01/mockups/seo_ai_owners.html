One owner for each piece of work: SEO versus AI answers

Where a change lands decides who owns it. Each piece of work is charged once, even when it helps both search and AI answers.

Brand Visibility services

AI answers

  • L01, L02: prompt strategy, setup and baseline
  • O01: the one monthly readout; it picks the month's edge fixes, and in the AEM play carries the search section from SO2
  • L03: edge pilot; C02: product pages, for AI assistants
  • O04, O07, O08, O09: off-site work
Adobe: Brand Visibility's edge fixes reach only ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude. People and search engines get the original page (Optimize at Edge overview, Aug 26, 2026). So these fixes aren't SEO work. Brand Visibility still uses SEO data (Semrush, Search Console) to pick what to fix.
AEM play: Sites Optimizer and the site build

Search

  • SO1: setup, roles, Search Console in Sites Optimizer
  • SO2: the month's site fixes in one allowance (up to 10 at Typical): Sites Optimizer's own fixes, plus the site fixes Brand Visibility flags (robots.txt rules for AI crawlers, redirects for pages AI agents can't reach)
  • Before launch, A02 (the Edge Delivery migration, not a Sites Optimizer service): titles, descriptions, structured data and redirects. Sites Optimizer needs a public production site, so SO1 and SO2 start after launch.
Adobe: Sites Optimizer puts its fixes into the page through AEM authoring, where people publish them, and sends page-speed code fixes as pull requests for developers to merge (Deploying to author, Aug 19, 2026). Because the page itself changes, Google and every assistant see the fix.

Retires: Technical SEO and AI-search improvements (O03). It put SEO under Brand Visibility, and in the AEM play it could charge Sites Optimizer's fixes a second time ($8,400 a month at Typical hours and $150 an hour). Partners who sell a full SEO program add it as their own service.
